## Context

Axiom Academic's website runs on **Tilda**: great for publishing fast, but
its SEO editor is just okay. Once you stack up dozens of pages, knowing which
ones have a clean title and description, a single H1 and a fast render
becomes a full-time job.

## The dashboard

A local app that:

- **Scrapes every published page** on `axiom-academic.com` continuously
- Extracts the title, meta description, H1, canonicals and OG tags
- Measures load time and a simplified Lighthouse score
- Flags **duplicates** (two pages with the same title, two identical H1s…)
- List view, filters, sorting by score

## Why a side project

Strictly tied to Axiom Academic (maintenance), but self-contained enough to
have its own logic, its own UI, its own pace. A tool I could one day take to
other Tilda sites if needed.
