## The context

Summer 2016, Moscow. I was living there and had fallen for **fixies**: those
fixed-gear bikes, no derailleur, no front brake. The original idea: open a
shop to sell them.

Picture Moscow in 2016: hardly anyone on the streets on a bike, let alone a
fixie. Back then, cycling wasn't really a way to get around the city.

## What I did instead

The Instagram account. I'd go out and photograph every bike I liked, and lots
of fixies, because there was a **small underground hype** around them in
Moscow and Saint Petersburg. Bits of a scene you'd stumble upon by chance, in
courtyards, outside bars.

The shop never opened. The account stayed on as a trace of that idea, and of
that city, at that moment.
