## The pitch

Parcoursup helps French high-school students choose a course based on what
exists today. Parcoursoup wants to help them choose based on what might
exist tomorrow.

"You're choosing studies today that will end in 2031. But do you know what
the job market will look like in 2031?"

The site itself is in French, like the students it's written for.

## What's inside

- **982 degrees** from Parcoursup 2026, i.e. 25,793 course offerings, linked
  to the **1,923 jobs** of France's official job classification (ROME)
  through the national register of qualifications.
- For each job, its **exposure to AI** measured on the real-world use of an
  AI assistant, **employers' hiring intentions** since 2019, what the State
  **planned for 2030** before ChatGPT, and **graduate employment** outcomes.
- A **future index out of 100** per course, four sub-scores, and verdicts
  with rules written out in black and white. No made-up probability, no
  odds: across 1,923 jobs, "AI can do it" and "employers are hiring less"
  aren't correlated, and the site shows both.
- A **weekly watch**, web and X, that logs announcements of agents, startups
  and job cuts affecting each job, with the source and a reliability score
  from 1 to 5.
- A comparison tool, a Method page that details every weighting and every
  limitation, a site written in plain language for a final-year student.

## How it's made

Ten public sources, all cited and dated: Parcoursup, RNCP, ROME, ESCO and
O*NET, the Anthropic Economic Index, France Travail's labour needs survey,
*Les Métiers en 2030*, InserSup, InserJeunes. The one link the official
tables don't cover, matching French jobs to American ones, is done by a
language model, with its confidence shown on every page.

Built in a single day, 27 October 2026, with Claude Code: data in Python, a
2,900-page static Astro site, deployed to Cloudflare Pages on every commit,
an automatic watch every Monday morning with a Telegram notification.

An independent, non-commercial site: no cookies, no account, no ads.
