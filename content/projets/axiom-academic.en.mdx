## The pitch

Axiom Academic connects families, mostly expats, with carefully selected
native French- and English-speaking private tutors all over the world. At
home or online, in the countries and cities where they live.

## What sets it apart

- **Demanding tutor sourcing** (interviews, tests, references).
- **Fully bilingual** FR/EN: we understand the school systems on both sides.
- **Closeness** with families: you talk to real humans, not a chatbot.
