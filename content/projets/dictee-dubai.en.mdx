## The event

Dubai's Giant Dictation (*la Dictée Géante*) took place on **Saturday
17 January 2026** at the AFLEC French International School. This edition's
theme: **family and passing things on**. More than 2,000 registered
participants of every level, from primary school children to adults. The
stated goal: **break the world record for the largest dictation ever held**.

## The set-up

- Free entry, open to everyone.
- Activities around the dictation: food trucks, French-language workshops
  open to beginners.
- A moment of francophonie in the heart of the Emirates.
