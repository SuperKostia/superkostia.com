## The story

Emma and me, Patmos, 9 to 12 July 2026. Guests from several countries, an
island with no airport, four days of celebration: everyone had to find
everything on their own. We wanted a website of our own, not a generic
template: programme, ferries and flights, where to stay, our island addresses,
a couple quiz, a shared playlist, a photo wall, a guestbook, a gift fund. In
French and English.

After the wedding, I turned it into an offer.

## The offer

[wedding.lifeispatmos.com](https://wedding.lifeispatmos.com/) builds the same
website, made to measure, for other couples, especially for **destination
weddings**, where the same questions come up a hundred times: which ferry,
where to stay, what time, what to wear.

- **A website in their names**, in their colours, in English and French.
- **Designed for phones**: guests open it on the ferry, on the beach, at
  midnight.
- **Updated until the big day**, and kept online after the party.
- **A real person** to talk to, not a form.

Our own website, now at
[emma-constantin.lifeispatmos.com](https://emma-constantin.lifeispatmos.com),
serves as the demo.

## How it ties in with Life is Patmos

It's an offer from [Life is Patmos](/projets/lifeispatmos), the local guide to
the island: same brand, same handwritten logo.
