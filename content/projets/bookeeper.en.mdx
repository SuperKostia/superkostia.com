## The story

One evening, with my friend Karim: *what if you could book a goalkeeper the
way you book an Uber?* The next day the prototype was live.

## The service

For amateur clubs, five-a-side or training teams who need a keeper for one
specific match:

- You enter your **area**, your **date**, your **level**.
- In a few clicks you book a **vetted and insured** goalkeeper.
- You pay **only for the hours played**.
