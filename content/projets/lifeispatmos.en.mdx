## The idea

I spend a lot of time on Patmos. **Life is Patmos** was born in 2020 as a
small shop of souvenirs made on the island: T-shirts, pouches and beach bags
sewn by local seamstresses, postcards, posters.

In October 2026 I rebuilt it from scratch as a **local guide**: the places
we go back to every summer, hand-picked by people who love the island. It's
the pilot for a "Life is…" brand that could expand to other Greek islands.

## What you'll find

- **45 places** in five sections: where to eat, beaches, sights, drinks,
  where to wander, plus a search.
- **Our favourites**: Chora, the Monastery of Saint John, Psili Ammos, Livadi
  Geranou…
- **An island shop**: the original Life is Patmos goods, and room for the
  merchants and makers who want to sell theirs. Order online, pick up on the
  island or have it delivered.
- **Practical info** for getting there: no airport, you arrive by sea.
- **Wedding websites** made to measure for couples marrying on the island or
  elsewhere: [Life is Patmos Weddings](/projets/wedding-patmos).

## How it works

Business owners can **claim their listing** and manage it themselves, with a
sign-in link sent by email: they only see their own listings and products.
The site is in English by default, with a French version.

Next.js and Payload CMS in the same codebase, a Postgres database on Neon,
photos on Vercel Blob. Built with Claude Code.
