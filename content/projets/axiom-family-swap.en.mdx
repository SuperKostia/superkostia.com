## The pitch

A language and cultural exchange programme **between families**. A child
spends 1 to 2 weeks with a host family abroad, and the host family sends
theirs back at another time. The home becomes the classroom, the country
becomes the school.

> Give your children the world as their school.

## What's different from a classic language trip

- **No middleman agency**: families choose each other directly through their
  profiles (country, children's ages, languages spoken, interests).
- **Reciprocity**: no accommodation fee, it's an exchange, not a paid
  service. The platform stays **100% free** for verified families.
- **Full immersion**: real family routine, local school, the host child's
  friends. Worlds away from supervised group trips that keep foreigners
  among themselves.

## The Axiom ecosystem

Axiom Family Swap is a satellite of [Axiom Academic](/projets/axiom-academic):
the same belief that learning a language happens through everyday life and
human connection, not textbooks. Families already working with Axiom tutors
find a natural next step for the school holidays.
