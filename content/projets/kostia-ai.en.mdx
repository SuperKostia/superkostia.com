## The pitch

I write a lot: LinkedIn posts, a newsletter, notes. But many of the people who
follow me don't have time to read. So I built the site where all of it can be
heard: a notebook of thoughts out loud, on entrepreneurship, education,
artificial intelligence and life abroad.

## What's inside

- **Recordings** sorted by theme, with their origin (LinkedIn post or
  newsletter), their date and their length.
- **My AI-cloned voice**: every text is read by a copy of my voice, made with
  Gradium. It says so on every recording, plainly.
- **An English version of every text**, read by the same voice. It keeps my
  French accent, on purpose.
- **The text highlighted word by word** as you listen, like karaoke.
- A player that keeps playing from one page to the next, dotted waveforms, and a
  3D particle shape on the home page, picked at random on every visit.

## How it's made

Built with Claude Code in a few days. The site runs on a Cloudflare Worker
(Astro), the entries live in a D1 database and the audio files in R2. The
highlighting timings come from Gradium at generation time, or from an alignment
done locally with Whisper. An admin page lets me add, reorder and publish
recordings without touching the code.
